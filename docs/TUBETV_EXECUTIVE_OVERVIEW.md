# TubeTV — Executive Overview & Commercial Guide

**Platform:** [www.tube.co.ke](https://www.tube.co.ke)  
**Category:** Kenyan-First Mobile Streaming & Vertical Micro-Drama Platform  
**Audience:** Kenya, East & Sub-Saharan Africa, and the Global Diaspora

---

## 1. What is TubeTV?

**TubeTV** is a mobile-first streaming platform built for African storytelling. It combines the addictive, fast-paced vertical viewing style of TikTok with serialized television—leading with **Kenyan Vertical Micro-Dramas** (bite-sized, cliffhanger-driven episodes built for phones) alongside **Movies, Series, Shorts, Education, Farmers, Faith, Kids, Diaspora, and Live** programming.

Unlike traditional streaming platforms that force viewers to download heavy apps, create accounts, and enter credit cards before watching a single second, TubeTV works **instantly in any mobile browser** with zero signup required to start watching.

## 2. Core Capabilities & Viewer Experience

* **Instant Mobile Viewing (Zero Friction):** Viewers tap a link on TikTok, WhatsApp, or Instagram and start watching Episode 1 immediately—no app store download or registration wall.
* **Vertical Swipe Binge Player:** Full-screen portrait playback tailored for smartphones, with smooth episode-to-episode transitions, pre-loaded next episodes, and live **Cliffhanger Verdict Polls** that spark audience debate.
* **Smart Discovery & Retention:**
  * **For You Teaser Feed:** Endless discovery of show hooks.
  * **Continue Bingeing Bar:** Remembers where viewers stopped across sessions and devices.
  * **Daily Binge Streaks:** Rewards loyal viewers who check in daily with bonus free episode unlocks.
  * **Bilingual Support:** Full English and Kiswahili experience, plus Data Saver mode for mobile data networks.
* **Installable App Experience (PWA):** Viewers can add TubeTV directly to their phone's home screen with push notifications for new episode drops.

     * **24-Hour Pass:** KSh 10 (impulse cliffhanger unlock)
     * **Weekly Pass:** KSh 50

## 4. Built-In Marketing & Viral Growth Instruments

TubeTV turns every episode and every creator into a customer-acquisition channel:

* **Rich Social Previews:** Every show and episode link automatically generates a custom poster, title, and synopsis preview when shared on WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, X, or Telegram.
* **Creator Viral Promo Link & Caption Generator:** Creators get one-click tracked links and pre-written English/Kiswahili captions for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp, with full traffic-source attribution in analytics.
* **Daily Push Alerts:** Automated evening push notifications alert subscribers when new episodes drop.

## 5. Creative & Filmmaker Features

TubeTV is built to be the most transparent and rewarding home for Kenyan and African filmmakers:

* **60/40 Creator-First Revenue Split:** Creators receive **60% of net revenue** (after standard payment gateway and platform fees).
* **Fair User-Centric Payouts:** Instead of lumping all money into one giant pool where big studios drown out indie creators, **each subscriber's pass money goes directly to the exact shows that subscriber watched**.
* **Ad-Host Earnings:** When a free viewer watches a 30-second sponsor ad to unlock a creator's episode, that creator earns **60% of the ad value**.
* **Self-Service Creator Portal & Automated Payouts:**
  * Transparent statements showing verified 30-second plays, watch time, and earnings.
  * Automatic balance rollover across months until the **KSh 1,000** minimum payout threshold is reached.
  * Direct disbursement via **M-Pesa B2C / Paystack Transfers**, with built-in tax/KRA PIN tracking and downloadable CSV statements.
* **Filmmaker Pitch & Submission Portal:** Independent creators can submit new shows directly through the platform for editorial review.
* **Creator Show Boosts:** Creators can promote their own trailers across other shows on TubeTV to acquire new fans.

## 7. Anti-Farming, Anti-Fraud & Anti-Download Protection

TubeTV protects both **creators' royalties** and **brands' ad budgets** with multiple layers of built-in security:

### Protecting Against View & Ad Farming
* **User-Centric Royalties:** A fake account or bot farm with a single KSh 10 pass can never drain the platform's royalty pool—its pass is capped at its own value.
* **Verified Watch Sessions:** Paid views only count toward royalties after a server-verified playback session confirms the viewer genuinely watched for at least 30 seconds (with a 1-play-per-viewer-per-day cap and a 90-minute maximum cap to stop idle looping).
* **Timed Ad Verification:** Sponsor ad completions and creator ad-host payouts are verified against real server wall-clock time—blocking scripts from faking ad views or draining sponsor budgets.
* **Single-Device Pass Lock:** Each paid pass works on **one active device at a time**. Logging in on a second phone automatically signs out the first, preventing password/pass sharing.

### Protecting Against Unauthorized Downloads & Piracy
* **Expiring Private Video Links:** Video streams are never public. Every play generates a private, cryptographically signed link that expires automatically (30 minutes for short episodes; up to 2 hours for full movies).
* **Direct File Blocking:** Raw video file downloads are blocked at the delivery network level, and external websites cannot embed or hotlink TubeTV streams.
* **Strict Admin & Payment Security:** Administrative publishing and payouts require two-factor authentication (authenticator app) with full audit trails, while automated background checks recover missed payment notifications every 30 minutes.
